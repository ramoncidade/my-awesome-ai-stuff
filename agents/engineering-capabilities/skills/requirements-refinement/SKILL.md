# Requirements Refinement

## Procedure
1. State the business or user outcome and intended audience.
2. Define scope and explicit non-goals.
3. Extract business rules, constraints, dependencies and actors.
4. Write testable acceptance criteria and relevant negative/edge cases.
5. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, inferences and open questions.
6. Identify risks and decisions that require human confirmation.
7. Trace each criterion to the source requirement where possible.

## Output
Objective, scope, non-goals, actors, business rules, constraints, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risks, assumptions and open questions.

## Safety
Never invent policy or business rules. Do not silently resolve contradictions. Mark unknowns rather than converting them into facts.
